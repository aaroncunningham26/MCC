Key Insights & Watch Items

Strength Watch Concern
  • Expenses outpacing income. Through the five complete months (Jan–May), operating expenses are up ~26% year-over-year ($791K vs. $628K) while operating income rose ~10% ($648K vs. $587K). The YTD operating result is a −$108,325 deficit. This gap is structural rather than seasonal — worth a line-item review with the new accounting firm.
  • Giving is behind budget pace. Average weekly giving is $28,631 (YTD giving $687,140 ÷ 24 weeks) against the $34,900 weekly budget — roughly 18% short. First-half giving is also tracking about 5% under last year's pace.
  • Personnel running high. Annualized personnel (5000) is ~$1.14M, about 62% of the $1.84M annual budget — above the 45–55% healthy range and the largest driver of the expense gap above.
  • Facilities in range. Annualized facilities (6200) is ~$332K, about 18% of budget — comfortably within the 15–25% guideline.
  • Cash is adequate but lean. Unrestricted cash is ~$385K (total bank $827K less ~$442K designated/restricted) ≈ 2.4 months of operating expense — acceptable, but thin alongside the $1.46M campaign loan.
  • Strong giving participation. 310 committed giving units (gave >$200 to 4100, trailing 12 mo) = 67.8% of 457 active households, with 36 new 4100 donors year-to-date.
  • Retention healthy, base slightly shrinking. 12-month retention is 74.1% (240 of 324 prior committed units), but 84 lapsed vs. 70 newly committed — a net −14 committed units, with only 1 new donor in the past week as summer giving slows.
  • Summer trough underway. June–August is historically MCC's giving low point; December typically surges to roughly 2× a normal month. Expect softer summer receipts and plan cash accordingly.
