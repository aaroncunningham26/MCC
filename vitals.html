Key takeaways

What this data is telling us

  • Weekend attendance is at an all-time high — 1,059 average through Week 17, up 4% from 2025. The church has grown meaningfully every year since 2022.
  • Adults in circles continues to trend positively — 593 adults (66%) are in a circle, the highest on record. The 70% goal is within reach.
  • Attendance growth (4%) is just under the 5% minimum goal, and regular serving (47%) and unique donors (22%) are both below target. These three are the key watch areas for 2026.
  • Student ministry is the most concerning trend — average students has declined from 103 in 2024 to 74 in 2026, dropping from 13% to 8% of attendance. This is consistently below the 10–15% goal and worsening.
  • Unique donors (231, 22%) remain well below the 40–60% goal. The donor base relative to attendance has contracted significantly since 2022 (44%). This is a long-term giving health indicator worth monitoring closely alongside the financial dashboard.
  • Count-based metrics (visitors, baptisms, Connect Breakfast, giving total) are YTD through Week 17 and should not be compared directly to prior full-year totals. Averages (attendance, serving, circles) are fully comparable.
