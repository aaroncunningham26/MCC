Key takeaways

What this data is telling us

  • Attendance is strong and climbing — the June weekly average hit 1,151, the highest month of the year, pulling the YTD average up to 1,042. Weekly averages have risen every year (942 → 977 → 1,122 → 1,151).
  • Adult engagement is healthy — average weekly adult attendance has grown from 798 (2023) to 982, and group serving now runs at 54–58% of adult/student attendance.
  • Sunday visitors are tracking behind — 2026 YTD is 186 (18% of attendance) against a 100% goal. Prior years landed 57–70%, so guest volume relative to attendance is the key watch area.
  • Next-steps pathways are quieter this year — Starting Point (15) and baptisms (16) are YTD counts through June and are pacing below recent full years. Both are cumulative, so they will grow through the fall.
  • Life / Care / Legacy group membership is steady at ~575–615 members (42–46% of adult/student attendance), holding the discipleship base as attendance grows.
