Key takeaways

What this data is telling us

  • Weekend attendance is healthy — the YTD weekly average is 1,042, with a 6-week moving average of 981. Mar 29 was the strongest week of the year at 1,421.
  • June averaged 956 per weekend—below the YTD pace, consistent with the normal early-summer dip.
  • Discipleship base is strong — 612 adults are in a Life/Care/Legacy group (~59% of adults) and 387 serve on Sundays.
  • The roster stands at 1,517 active profiles across 470 households (1,029 adults, 155 students, 333 kids), with 33 new Sunday guests in the last month.
  • Two metrics need a live source: baptisms and Connect Breakfast aren't currently maintained in Planning Center, so they show as not tracked. Point them at a real list or the Sheet to light them up.
