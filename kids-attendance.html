Key takeaways

What this data is telling us

  • A typical Maple City Kids family is here about 2–3 Sundays a month. Even the core (families active 9+ of the last 12 months) averages 2.6, not 4.
  • Among the 146 regular families, nearly 9 in 10 average between 1.5 and 3.5 Sundays a month. Only 9 families average 3.5 or more.
  • Once you account for month length, attendance is steady at 52–58% of Sundays most months. The softest month was July (47%). Nov–Dec ran higher (~65%) because those months had only 3 programmed Sundays, and the five-Sunday months (Mar, May, Aug) post the highest raw averages.
  • Planning implication: a family that is “regular” will miss roughly half of any given series. Kids’ curriculum and volunteer rotations work best when each Sunday stands on its own.

How this is calculated

Data: every attendee check-in (regular + guest, volunteers excluded) to the Maple City Kids event in Planning Center Check-Ins, Nov 2, 2025 – Oct 25, 2026.

Family: a Planning Center household. Siblings on the same Sunday count once. Children listed in two households (e.g., shared custody) link those households into one family. 6 unlinked one-time guest check-ins are excluded.

Sundays: 49 Sundays with kids programming. Nov 9, Nov 30 and Dec 28, 2025 had no kids check-ins and are treated as no-programming Sundays.

Averages: “Months attended” averages only months where the family came at least once. “Full year” divides each family’s total Sundays by 12.
